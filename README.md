# 🚀 MirageMind AI

### Autonomous Environmental Intelligence Platform for Habitability Assessment & Exploration

MirageMind AI is an AI-powered environmental intelligence system designed to analyze environmental conditions and estimate the possibility of life in unknown or extreme environments. The project combines robotics, sensor fusion, embedded systems, and intelligent decision-making to support autonomous exploration missions on Earth and beyond.

---

## 🌟 Project Vision

Future exploration missions require autonomous systems capable of evaluating environmental conditions without continuous human intervention.

MirageMind AI aims to:

- Collect multi-sensor environmental data
- Analyze habitability indicators
- Detect environmental risks
- Estimate life-supporting conditions
- Assist autonomous exploration systems

The long-term vision is to integrate MirageMind AI into intelligent robotic platforms for planetary exploration, disaster-response missions, and remote-environment monitoring.

## ⚙ System Architecture

MirageMind AI consists of:

1. Sensor Acquisition Layer
2. ESP32 Data Processing Unit
3. Sensor Fusion Layer
4. Habitability Assessment Engine
5. Decision Engine
6. Visualization Dashboard

The system evaluates:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Air Quality
- Light Intensity
- Obstacle Detection

to generate a Life Possibility Score and Environmental Classification.

## 📊 Life Possibility Assessment

MirageMind AI calculates a Life Possibility Score using:

- Temperature Conditions
- Humidity Levels
- Air Quality Measurements
- Light Intensity

Environmental status is classified as:

- Hostile Environment
- Potentially Habitable
- Habitable Environment
